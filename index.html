01 / ABOUT

I study how archives, records, and memory are made meaningful across institutional, community, and platform environments. My current work examines recordkeeping and digital nostalgia, lost media and vernacular archival practice, archives and affect, archival advocacy, and AI-generated records.

I am currently based at the School of Information Sciences at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign as a Scholar-in-Residence. I am completing my PhD in Archival Studies at Renmin University of China.

02 / RESEARCH
R.01

Digital nostalgia & mediated recordkeeping

How people create, collect, organize, curate, and circulate nostalgic records through platform cultures and internet microgenres, including dreamcore.

R.02

Lost media & vernacular archival practice

Community-led discovery, evidence-making, provenance work, reconstruction, and preservation in Lost Media Wikis and related participatory environments.

R.03

Archival advocacy, activism & discourse

The language, institutional conditions, and practical boundaries through which archival actors seek support, shape agendas, and intervene in life.

R.04

AI-generated records & trust

Emerging forms of records produced through AI systems, with particular attention to provenance, trustworthiness, perception, and evidentiary value.
